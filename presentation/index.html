PHASE SCIENCE OPERATIONS · SYSTEM POWER

CAUSEReduced solar generation, on a power system with too little margin to absorb it.

SYMPTOMBus power falls below the demand of the systems that are on.

The team must respond
OPTION A
Suspend the instruments

The spacecraft stays safe, but this period produces no science.

OPTION B
Activate power-saving mode

Keeps part of the science, in proportion to the power left.

OPTION C
Continue normal operations

Demand stays above supply: safe mode, no downlink this period.

Decision recorded. Instrument time is cut to fit the power available; observations and data fall in the same proportion. The debrief later compares the result with a no-anomaly reference.
Illustrative sequence. The V4 engine’s anomaly module (MCAS) models six Earth-observation anomalies — communication outage, power shortage, data backlog, instrument unavailable, pointing instability, optical-downlink cloud — each with two or three responses.
